Protect the work

Aftercare, in order

Keep it wrapped

Leave the bandage on as long as your artist told you — usually 2 to 24 hours depending on the wrap.

Wash gently

Clean hands, lukewarm water, fragrance-free soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel — never rub.

Moisturize thin

A rice-grain amount of fragrance-free lotion, 2–3 times a day. More is not better.

Protect for 2 weeks

No pools, ocean, tanning, or picking. Sun is the enemy of every tattoo — SPF for life once healed.

Straight answers

Common questions

How does pricing work?
Every piece is priced individually based on size, detail, and placement. You'll get a clear quote at your consult before anything is scheduled — no surprises in the chair.
Is there a shop minimum?
Yes — like every professional studio, we have a shop minimum that covers fresh needles, inks, and full setup for even the smallest piece. Your artist will tell you the current minimum when you book.
Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome every day for smaller pieces and flash, first come first served. Custom work books through the request card so your artist can draw for you ahead of time.
How do deposits work?
A deposit confirms your appointment and comes off the final price of your tattoo. It covers your artist's drawing time, so it's non-refundable if you no-show — reschedules with notice are no problem.
What do I bring?
Valid government ID (18+, no exceptions), a full stomach, comfortable clothes that expose the placement, and any reference images.
Do you do touch-ups?
Yes — small touch-ups on our work are free within the first few months when the piece was cared for per aftercare. Just get in touch.
Does it hurt?
Yes, somewhat — anyone who says otherwise is selling something. Placement matters more than size. Your artist will be straight with you about your spot.

Before you visit

Good to know

Walk-ins run first come, first served — earlier in the day means less wait. Custom work always starts with the booking card so your artist can draw before you sit.

Eat a real meal first, skip the pre-tattoo drinks (we can't tattoo you otherwise), and bring one friend max — chairs are for clients.
